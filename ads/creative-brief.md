## Calls to action & landing pages

* Button: **Get a quote** (or *Call now* on Reels where the button is less prominent).
* Send paid traffic to the homepage quote form — it's one screen with phone, address and
  optional photo upload, which is the whole conversion. Don't split traffic across the
  service sub-pages until you're spending enough for statistical significance.
* Add a **click-to-call** on the ad itself: (808) 927-6202. Many of these customers will call
  rather than fill a form, especially the commercial ones.

---

## Creative notes / things to verify

* Badges on the videos say **"5.0 on Google"**, **"locally owned since 2020"** and
  **"island-wide"** — all from your current site copy; confirm before scaling spend.
* Ad 10's scarcity line and ad 7's "same-day quotes" are promises — only run them when true.
* `crew`, `garage`, `08` and `09` show identifiable team members. Get their OK before paid
  distribution.
* If you get a real review or a dramatic before/after photo, swap it into ad 9 or 2 and
  re-render — social proof is the cheapest lift you have.
